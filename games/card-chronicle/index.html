How to play

  • You have six attempts. Each attempt includes a year and a price; both must be correct in the same attempt to win.
  • The year is the English set release year for this printing, not the Pokemon's debut or the copyright date.
  • The full card is shown with the bottom copyright-year line blurred until the puzzle ends. The name, artwork, and other printed details are available as clues.
  • Guess the ungraded, non-foil TCGplayer market price in USD. The specific edition is shown when the answer is revealed.
  • The year must be exact. Price guesses within 10% of the answer, or $0.25 (whichever is larger), count as correct. Boundaries are inclusive.
  • Each guess tells you whether the answer is higher or lower. A correct result is labeled "Correct" so clues don't rely on color.
  • Everyone gets the same card each day, resetting at midnight UTC. Your progress is saved in this browser when storage is available.
  • Use "Practice / test cards" to choose among the starter cards, advance to the next one, or restart with six fresh attempts. Return to "Daily challenge" to resume your daily progress.

Starter beta: a small rotating card collection with fixed price snapshots, not live quotes. Cards repeat while the collection grows. The source's price update date is shown with each answer.
