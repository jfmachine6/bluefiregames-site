How to play

  • You have six attempts. Each attempt includes a year and a price; both must be correct in the same attempt to win.
  • The year is the English set release year for this printing, not the Pokemon's debut or the copyright date.
  • The full card is shown with the bottom copyright-year line blurred until the puzzle ends. The name, artwork, and other printed details are available as clues.
  • Guess the ungraded TCGplayer market price in USD for the exact edition and finish pictured and labeled above the inputs. A first-edition stamp means a first-edition price, never an Unlimited substitute. Holo, non-foil, and special parallel versions are not interchangeable.
  • Every selected card has a market snapshot of at least $1. We intentionally balance five price bands: $1–$5, $5–$20, $20–$75, $75–$250, and $250+. Upper limits are exclusive. Higher bands appear much more often than in a random pack or bulk collection.
  • The year must be exact. Price guesses within 10% of the answer, or $0.25 (whichever is larger), count as correct. Boundaries are inclusive.
  • Each guess tells you whether the answer is higher or lower. A correct result is labeled "Correct" so clues don't rely on color.
  • Everyone gets the same card each day, resetting at midnight UTC. Your progress is saved in this browser when storage is available.
  • Use "Practice (20)" for a separate set of 20 cards available immediately. Advance to the next card or restart with six fresh attempts. Completed rounds are saved; unfinished practice is in-tab only. Return to "Daily" to resume daily progress.
  • Use "Archive / catch up" to play old days or review your results. Archived attempts are saved by puzzle date, not the date you played them. Completed practice rounds keep separate records of guesses and original card/price snapshots.

Fixed price snapshots, not live quotes. The source's update date appears with each answer. The beta starter puzzles were replaced; the new daily schedule has its own browser progress. English cards begin in 1999, not 1995.
